ABOUT US

Takshasila Centre for Policy Research and Innovation (TCPRI)

Takshasila Centre for Policy Research and Innovation (TCPRI) is an independent policy research think tank committed to advancing evidence-based research, strategic analysis, and innovative policy solutions.

Our research focuses on geopolitics, international relations, public policy, governance, climate change, and global economic affairs. We seek to examine emerging national and international challenges, generate meaningful insights, and contribute to informed decision-making.

Through rigorous research, constructive dialogue, and collaboration, TCPRI aims to bridge the gap between ideas and policy, fostering a deeper understanding of the forces shaping India and the world.

We believe that informed analysis and innovative thinking are essential to building effective, inclusive, and forward-looking public policies.

Our Vision: To contribute to a more informed, resilient, and progressive society through research-driven policy insights.

Our Approach: Research. Analyse. Innovate. Impact.

Follow TCPRI as we explore emerging policy developments, examine global challenges, and contribute to meaningful discussions on the future of governance and international affairs.
